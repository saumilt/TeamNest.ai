Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 June 2026 · Last updated: 1 June 2026

TeamNest.ai (“TeamNest”, “we”, “us”) provides an AI‑native team communication, research and app‑building platform available on the web and as iOS & Android apps. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have.

By using TeamNest, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, and (optionally) phone number and profile photo, used to create and secure your account.
  • Workspace & content — messages, chats, files and attachments you upload, tasks, AI research threads, projects and templates you create or interact with.
  • Payment information — when you purchase credits, subscriptions or templates, payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card details; Stripe handles card data under its own policies.
  • Usage & device data — basic diagnostics such as app version, device type, and error logs, used to keep the service reliable and secure.
  • Permissions you grant — on mobile, we access your photo library only when you choose to attach an image. We do not access your camera, contacts, or location.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, operate and improve TeamNest and its features.
  • To deliver AI features you request (e.g. AI chat, multi‑model research, @devmanager builds).
  • To process payments and manage your subscription/credits.
  • To communicate with you about your account, security, and service updates.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

3. AI processing & third‑party sub‑processors

To power AI features, content you submit to an AI feature (such as your prompt or attached files) may be sent to third‑party AI model providers strictly to generate a response for you. We use reputable providers, which may include OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. We also use:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • OpenAI / Anthropic / Google — AI model inference for chat, research and building features.
  • Mailgun — transactional email (e.g. invites, account notices).
  • Cloud hosting & database providers used to run the service.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

4. How we share information

  • Within your workspace — content you post is visible to other members of the chats/workspaces you belong to.
  • Service providers — the sub‑processors listed above, only as needed to run the service.
  • Legal — where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of users and the public.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, with notice where required.

5. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. When you delete content or your account, we delete or de‑identify the associated personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal, security, or accounting reasons.

6. Deleting your account & data

You can delete your account at any time directly in the app: go to You → Delete account (or in the web app, your profile settings). This permanently removes your personal account data. You may also email us to request deletion.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls, and hashed credentials to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using industry‑standard measures.

8. Children

TeamNest is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location (e.g. GDPR/EEA, UK, CCPA/California), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

10. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and revise the “Last updated” date. Material changes will be communicated where appropriate.
