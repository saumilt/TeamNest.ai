## Description (iOS & Play — full)

**TeamNest.ai is the AI‑native workspace for modern teams.** Chat with your teammates and with AI in the very same thread, get one clear answer from every top model at once, and turn conversations into shipped work — all from a fast, beautiful mobile app.

**💬 Team chat, reimagined**
Real‑time group and direct messages with files, mentions, and an AI assistant built into every workspace. Your whole team on the same page.

**✦ Ask once. Compare every model.**
Run ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Perplexity and Grok in parallel and get a single synthesized answer — no more copy‑pasting between apps or wondering which model is right.

**@ai and @devmanager, right in the conversation**
Mention @ai to research or draft inside any chat. Mention @devmanager to turn a discussion into a real, working app — TeamNest’s AI dev team plans, builds, and ships it.

**✓ Turn talk into done**
Capture action items from any chat and track them by priority and due date, so nothing slips.

**🛍 Template Marketplace**
Start from ready‑made app templates — CRMs, help desks, booking systems and more — and make them yours.

**🔒 Private & in your control**
Manage your profile, workspaces and privacy. Delete your account and data any time, right from the app.

Download TeamNest.ai and give your team AI superpowers.
